# Alongside

**Understand your child. Find your way.**

Alongside is a prototype app for parents and carers in Tower Hamlets whose child, aged 18 months to 11, may be
neurodivergent. It brings together two projects:

- **Holding Hands** by Thennarasu Sivakumar: an observation guide and a dated record of what a parent notices,
  which turns into one page for a meeting.
- **A-Pass** by Tsz Ching Chan: a map of the three roads to help (healthcare, SEN Support and EHC plans),
  stage by stage, with who you will meet and what to ask.

MA Service Design, London College of Communication (UAL), with THCVS and parents, SENCOs and professionals in
Tower Hamlets.

## Use it

Open the link on a phone. To keep it like an app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share, then *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** the menu, then *Install app* or *Add to Home screen*.

It works without a signal after the first visit.

## Privacy

- No account, no server, no analytics, no cookies, and no requests to any other website.
- Everything a parent writes, photographs or films stays on their own phone, in that browser.
- *Log out* in the profile menu deletes it from the phone.
- The app gives no scores, ratings or predictions, and is not a screening or diagnostic tool.
